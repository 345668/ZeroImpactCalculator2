### **Updated Carbon Credit Calculation Prompt (Including 10-Year Prognosis)**  

#### **Title:** Carbon Credit Calculation with 10-Year Prognosis  

#### **Prompt:**  
Develop a structured **carbon credit calculation model** based on energy efficiency improvements over a **10-year period**. The user provides **pre-renovation and post-renovation energy data**, and the system calculates **CO₂ savings, carbon credits, and financial value**.  

---

### **Required Calculation Steps:**

#### **1. Convert Energy Source to kWh**
Each heating source has a specific energy content per unit. Convert the given **fuel consumption** to kWh:  

| **Energy Source** | **Unit** | **Conversion to kWh** |
|------------------|--------|--------------------|
| **Gas**         | m³     | **1 m³ = 10.4 kWh** |
| **Oil**         | Liter  | **1 L = 9.4 kWh**  |
| **Pellet**      | kg     | **1 kg = 4.8 kWh** |

\[
\text{Energy (kWh)} = \text{Energy Amount} \times \text{Conversion Factor}
\]

---

#### **2. Calculate CO₂ Emissions for the Current Heating System**
After conversion to kWh, use the **correct emission factor** for the energy source:

| **Energy Source** | **Emission Factor** (kg CO₂/kWh) |
|------------------|--------------------------|
| **Gas**         | 0.24                      |
| **Oil**         | 0.30                      |
| **Pellet**      | 0.10                      |
| **Electricity Mix** | 0.343                  |

\[
\text{Current CO₂ Emissions (kg)} = \text{Energy (kWh)} \times \text{Emission Factor}
\]

Multiply by **10 years** to compute the total CO₂ emissions **over a decade**:

\[
\text{Current CO₂ Emissions (kg, 10 years)} = \text{Current CO₂ Emissions (kg)} \times 10
\]

---

#### **3. Calculate CO₂ Emissions for the New Heating System**
- If switching to a **heat pump**, assume the system consumes electricity.  
- Convert **post-renovation energy consumption** (in kWh) using the **electricity mix emission factor (0.343 kg CO₂/kWh)**.

\[
\text{Projected CO₂ Emissions (kg)} = \text{Electricity Consumption (kWh)} \times 0.343
\]

Multiply by **10 years** to compute the emissions for the full prognosis:

\[
\text{Projected CO₂ Emissions (kg, 10 years)} = \text{Projected CO₂ Emissions (kg)} \times 10
\]

---

#### **4. Compute Carbon Credits (CO₂ Savings)**
Calculate the CO₂ saved by subtracting projected emissions from current emissions:

\[
\text{Carbon Credits (t CO₂, 10 years)} = \frac{\text{Current CO₂ Emissions (kg, 10 years)} - \text{Projected CO₂ Emissions (kg, 10 years)}}{1000}
\]

---

#### **5. Calculate the Financial Value of Carbon Credits**
Carbon credits are **valued at €50 per ton of CO₂**:

\[
\text{Value of Carbon Credits (€)} = \text{Carbon Credits (t CO₂, 10 years)} \times 50
\]

---

### **Additional Requirements:**
- **Numerical Formatting:** The calculation should be formatted according to the selected **language (`de` or `en`)**:
  - **German (`de`)**: `,` as decimal separator, `.` for thousands (`63,56 t CO₂`).
  - **English (`en`)**: `.` as decimal separator, `,` for thousands (`63.56 t CO₂`).

- **Input Parameters:**
  - **Language Selection** (`de` or `en`).
  - **Current Heating System & Fuel Type** (Gas, Oil, Pellet).
  - **Annual Energy Consumption (before renovation)**.
  - **Projected Energy Consumption (after renovation)**.
  - **New Heating System Type** (e.g., Heat Pump, Gas Condensing Boiler, etc.).
  - **Carbon Credit Price per Ton (default: €50 per ton, but adjustable).**

- **Output Data (10-Year Prognosis):**
  - **Current CO₂ Emissions (kg & tons, 10 years).**
  - **Projected CO₂ Emissions (kg & tons, 10 years).**
  - **Total Carbon Credits (tons CO₂ saved, 10 years).**
  - **Total Financial Value (€).**
  - **Formatted Output Consistent with the Selected Language.**
